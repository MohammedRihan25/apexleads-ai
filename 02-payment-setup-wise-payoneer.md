# Frictionless Global USD Payment Setup SOP
## How to Receive USD/EUR/GBP from India with Zero Gateway Stress

Traditional payment gateways (like Stripe or PayPal) often freeze accounts or require weeks of documentation. For our B2B Productized Service, we use **direct bank transfer rails (ACH / Wire / SEPA)** and **Merchant of Record (MoR)** platforms.

---

### Option A: Wise Business / Wise Personal (Recommended)
**Wise (formerly TransferWise)** is the #1 tool used by remote agencies and freelancers globally.

#### Why It Works:
1. When you sign up, Wise provides you with your own **US Bank Account details**:
   * **Account Number**
   * **Routing Number (ABA / ACH)**
   * **Bank Name:** (Usually Community Federal Savings Bank or Evolve Bank & Trust, NY)
2. To your US client, paying you looks just like paying a local American company via standard ACH bank transfer or debit/credit card.
3. Once the client sends $397, Wise automatically converts it at the mid-market exchange rate and deposits INR straight into your Indian bank account (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, etc.) within **12 to 24 hours**.

#### Setup Steps:
1. Go to `wise.com` or download the Wise app.
2. Register an account with your Indian phone number, PAN card, and Aadhaar.
3. Open a **USD currency balance**.
4. Click **Get account details** for USD.
5. Save your Routing Number and Account Number to paste into client invoices.

---

### Option B: Payoneer Global Payment Service
If Wise is not immediately accessible in your region, **Payoneer** is the battle-tested alternative backed by RBI for Indian exports.

#### Setup Steps:
1. Go to `payoneer.com` and sign up for an Individual / Freelancer account.
2. Complete KYC by submitting your PAN card and Indian bank account details (account number + IFSC).
3. Under **Receive** -> **Global Payment Service**, Payoneer generates approved US (First Century Bank), UK (Barclays), and EU (Citibank) bank details.
4. Use Payoneer's **"Request a Payment"** feature: you type in the client's email, and Payoneer emails them a credit card / ACH payment link!

### Option C: Lemon Squeezy Invoice Link (Instant Card Checkout)
If the client wants to pay via **Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Credit Card** immediately:
1. Create a free account on `lemonsqueezy.com`.
2. Lemon Squeezy acts as the **Merchant of Record (MoR)**.
3. Create a one-time product titled: *"Custom B2B Lead List & Outreach Campaign — 500 Verified Leads"* priced at `$397`.
4. Copy the payment link and send it in the DM/email.
5. When the client clicks, they pay with Apple Pay / Card in 5 seconds. Lemon Squeezy sends your payouts directly to your Indian bank or Wise account.
